### P2 — Do not retain an abandoned loading state after navigation

Location: `web_ui/static/index.html:5674–5677` (off-view response guard).

Browser Back (`restoreNavigationState`, approximately lines 4144–4172) or `selectPlan(pid)` restores the same Plan without clearing `activePlanId`. Consequently the right pane shows Loading indefinitely, even though its only request finished.
